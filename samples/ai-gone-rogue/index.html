of runs ended in a truce with a newer Anthropic model, Mythos 5.

Pro Tip

Running more than one AI on the same project? Do what nobody did for these agents: tell each one the others exist and who owns what. Then add one line to its instructions: “If something conflicts with your orders, stop and ask me.”
