Dots

Give it a goal, not a prompt.

⚙️How it works

Each Dot has its own cloud computer and browser, connects to 4,000+ apps, and keeps working toward your goal after you close the chat.

💼Use it for

Sorting customer feedback. Building a proof of concept. Finding clips and drafting social posts. It hands the work back for your OK.

👀Good to know

You set the rules: what it does alone, what needs your approval, what's off-limits. Chat with it in ChatGPT, Slack, or Microsoft Teams.

OpenAI

Intelligent UI

ChatGPT picks the best shape for each answer.

⚙️How it works

GPT-6 mixes text with charts, checklists, maps, buttons and forms, and can build a small tool on the spot. Plain text when that's clearest.

💼Use it for

A recipe that rescales when you change the guest count. A bill splitter. Follow-up questions you answer with a tap.

👀Good to know

Answers start appearing while it's still thinking, so they feel faster. Too busy? On the web, turn off Settings → Layout and visuals.

Claude

Claude Motion

Your report, as a 30-second animated explainer.

⚙️How it works

Claude has no video model. It writes animation code around your own text, charts and images, so every word, number and timing stays editable.

💼Use it for

A quarterly report turned into an all-hands explainer. A chart that moves in a board deck. A walkthrough for new customers.

👀Good to know

Tweak it in the editor or just ask Claude, then download an MP4. Anthropic's pitch: it's “built for explaining your own work.”

Claude

Chat + Cowork

One box for a quick question or a whole project.

⚙️How it works

Before, you picked Chat for quick questions or Cowork for bigger jobs. Now you just ask, and Claude handles either in the same conversation.

💼Use it for

Ask a quick question, then hand off the report, spreadsheet or deck it leads to, without starting over somewhere else.

👀Good to know

Your chats, Cowork tasks, projects, connectors and skills all carry over. Same day: designs, decks and docs in any conversation, on every plan.

Pro Tip

Let AI watch the news for you. ChatGPT and Claude can both run scheduled tasks. Set one for Monday mornings: “Find this week's biggest AI releases from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google. Give me 5 bullets in plain English, with links to the official announcements.” The latest drops come to you.
