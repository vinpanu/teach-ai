Spot the difference.

        

          

            
❌ Old: what goes wrong

            
You MUST… NEVER… CRITICAL!!!
Shouting. Newer AI takes it too literally and overreacts.

            
STEP 1… STEP 2… STEP 12
Every step scripted. The AI can't take a better route than yours.

            
=== WEEKLY FEEDBACK REPORT ===
Rigid template. No space for what actually matters this week.

            
Do NOT add any other sections.
No “why.” When something unexpected shows up, the AI has no way to adapt.

          

          

            
✅ New: why it works

            
Goal: Summarize this week's emails…
Calm, clear goal. One sentence says what you want.

            
Why it matters: 15 minutes to decide…
Explains why. The AI can make smart calls on its own.

            
What I know: new login page on Tuesday
Shares context. Facts it couldn't guess, so it can connect the dots.

            
Done = first line shows our #1 priority
Defines “great,” not the steps. The how is left to the AI.

            Pro Tip
            
When should you still be specific? When the exact format matters (a spreadsheet, a form, a legal clause) or the order of steps truly matters. Spell out the result you need, not every thought along the way.
