Pro Tip

After your AI edits a file, ask it to re-open the file and confirm the change is really there. “Done” should mean saved and checked, not “I think I saved it.”

Pro Tip

Keep testing independent. On POA, the agent that runs a test never sees the answer key, and a separate grader scores the results. Nobody marks their own homework.
